# ⚔️ Broadside - Dead Man's Tide

An atmospheric 2.5D naval combat and pirate adventure game playable in any modern desktop, mobile, or Smart TV web browser.

### 🎮 Play Now
👉 **[Play Broadside Online](https://jonathandeanlinden-tech.github.io/broadside-game/)**

### 🕹️ Controls

#### Keyboard & Mouse:
* **W / S**: Raise / Lower Sails (Speed)
* **A / D**: Steer Rudder
* **Q / E / SPACE**: Fire Cannons (Port / Starboard broadsides)
* **Shift + W**: Speed Dash surge (+25% burst, 10s cooldown)
* **X**: Drop Naval Mine
* **F**: Board Crippled Ships / Go Ashore on Islands
* **R**: Fish at water's edge
* **H**: Repair ship
* **Scroll Wheel**: Adjust Camera Zoom (<kbd>0</kbd> to reset)
* **Tab**: World Map
* **ESC**: Pause Menu

#### 📱 Smartphone Controller:
Open the game on your PC or Smart TV, and scan the QR code displayed on the screen with your smartphone camera to wirelessly connect your phone as the touchscreen gamepad controller!
